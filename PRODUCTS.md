# Legal Practice

All 7 products in this package.

Every entry below is taken from the package's product inventory. Nothing here is a plan or a
roadmap item; all of it is built.

---

## Estate Closer

14 files, 166 tests

Walks a family through settling a small estate: builds the checklist, tracks the deadlines, and drafts the letters and forms in the right order.

## Divorce Splitter

12 files, 170 tests

Reads financial documents, classifies each asset and debt as marital or separate under the applicable state's law, computes the net marital estate, and generates equitable-distribution proposals.

## Franchise Decoder

15 files, 155 tests

Reads a franchise disclosure document and tells a prospective buyer what they are actually agreeing to and what the numbers really say.

## Expert Witness Dossier

14 files, 120 tests

Builds a challenge package against an opposing expert: analyses their CV, publications and prior transcripts, then drafts the admissibility motions, deposition outlines and cross-examination material.

## PERM Visa Filer

10 files, 57 tests

Assembles a labour-certification package and the follow-on petition, offline, enforcing the government timing rules as it goes.

## Trademark Sentinel

13 files, 45 tests

Watches a trademark portfolio for confusingly similar filings and for approaching deadlines.

## Small Claims Packet

8 files, 34 tests

Builds a filing-ready small-claims packet from a plain description of what happened.
